Atiku Abubakar

Early Life

Atiku Abubakar was born on November 25, 1946, in Jada, Adamawa State, Nigeria. He grew up in the northern part of Nigeria and attended
primary and secondary schools in the region. Atiku developed an interest in education, public service, and
business from an early age. His experiences growing up later influenced his career in business and politics.

Education

Atiku Abubakar attended Jada Primary School and later attended Adamawa Provincial Secondary School. He subsequently studied at
the School of Hygiene in Kano and obtained a diploma. He later attended the Ahmadu Bello University Institute of
Administration, where he studied for a degree in Law but did not complete the programme.

Career

Atiku Abubakar began his working career in the Nigerian Customs Service in 1969. He worked there for many years and eventually
rose to become a Deputy Director before retiring in 1989. While working in the Customs Service, he also became involved in
business. He later built businesses in areas including logistics, agriculture, real estate, education, and media.

Atiku entered politics and became an important political figure in Nigeria. He served as Vice President of Nigeria from 1999 to 2007
under President Olusegun Obasanjo. During his time as Vice President, he was involved in economic and government reforms.

Achievements

Atiku Abubakar became one of Nigeria's most prominent politicians and businessmen. He served as Vice President of Nigeria between
1999 and 2007 and played a role in several government economic initiatives during that period.
He also established and supported educational institutions and various businesses. His investments have included areas such as
education, agriculture, logistics, and real estate.

Atiku has contested the Nigerian presidential election several times. He was the presidential candidate of the Peoples Democratic
Party (PDP) in the 2019 and 2023 presidential elections. Throughout his political career, he has remained an influential
figure in Nigerian politics and has continued to speak about economic development, education, employment, and national unity.
