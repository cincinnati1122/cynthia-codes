Bola Ahmed Tinubu

Early Life

Bola Ahmed Tinubu was born on March 29, 1952, in Osun State, Nigeria. He later moved to the United States, where he continued his education
and developed a career in accounting and finance. Tinubu developed an interest in public affairs and politics and later
returned to Nigeria, where he became actively involved in political activities.

Education

Bola Ahmed Tinubu attended St. John's Primary School in Aroloya, Lagos, and Children's Home School in Ibadan. He later travelled to the
United States and attended Richard J. Daley College in Chicago. He subsequently attended Chicago State University, where he studied
Accounting and graduated in 1979.

Career

After completing his education in the United States, Tinubu worked as an accountant for several American companies, including Arthur
Andersen and Deloitte. He returned to Nigeria and continued his career in finance before
becoming involved in politics. He was elected as a Senator representing the Lagos West constituency during Nigeria's Third Republic.

Tinubu later became Governor of Lagos State in 1999 and served two terms, from 1999 to 2007. During his time as governor, his
administration focused on areas such as infrastructure, taxation, transportation, urban development, and internally generated revenue.

Achievements

Bola Ahmed Tinubu became an influential figure in Nigerian politics. As Governor of Lagos State, he was associated with reforms in areas
such as public finance, transportation, taxation, and urban development.
He played a significant role in the formation and development of political organizations that later became part of the All
Progressives Congress (APC).

In 2023, Bola Ahmed Tinubu contested the Nigerian presidential election as the candidate of the All Progressives Congress. He won
the election according to the official results and was sworn in as President of Nigeria on May 29, 2023.
As President, his administration has focused on economic reforms, investment, infrastructure, security, and other national issues.
